🚨 Common UPI Scams

Learn the warning signs before you become a victim.

🧾

Fake Payment Screenshots

Fraudsters may send edited screenshots claiming that money has been transferred. A screenshot is not proof of payment.

📱

QR Code Scams

Scammers may tell you to scan a QR code to receive money. Be careful: approving a payment request can send money from your account.

🎣

Phishing & Fake Apps

Fake apps and links can imitate legitimate banking or payment services and attempt to steal sensitive information.

🔐 THE GOLDEN RULE

Your UPI PIN is used when you are making a payment. Never enter your UPI PIN, OTP or banking password because someone tells you that you need it to receive money.

🛡️ Protect Yourself

Simple habits can prevent many digital-payment scams.

👀 Verify Incoming Payments

Check your actual UPI application or bank account transaction history instead of trusting a screenshot sent by another person.

🔒 Protect Your PIN & OTP

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, password or other confidential banking credentials with anyone.

🚨 Don't Fall for Pressure

Messages such as "pay immediately" or "your money will be lost" can be manipulation tactics. Stop and verify first.

📲 Avoid Unknown Links

Be careful with suspicious SMS, email and social media links that ask you to provide banking or payment information.

🖥️ Don't Share Your Screen

Never give unknown people remote or screen-sharing access to your device to solve a supposed payment problem.

📞 Verify the Person

If someone contacts you about a payment, verify their identity through a trusted communication method before taking action.

💡 Remember

Verify before you trust. Think before you pay.

👁️

SEE

Look for unusual transaction IDs, inconsistent details and suspicious requests.

🧠

THINK

Don't allow urgency or pressure to make you approve a payment.

🛡️

Confirm the actual transaction inside your trusted UPI application.
