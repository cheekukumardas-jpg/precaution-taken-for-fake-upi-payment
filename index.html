🛡️ UPI Safety Rules

Always verify before approving a payment.

🔐

PIN = Payment

Your UPI PIN is used to authorize payments. Never enter it just because someone says you need it to receive money.

👀

Verify the Payee

Before approving a payment, carefully check the displayed recipient information.

🚨

Don't Rush

Urgency and pressure are common scam tactics. Stop and verify before paying.
